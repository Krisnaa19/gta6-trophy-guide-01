    The road to the platinum

    Seven stages, in order, built from the official trophy descriptions. Story first, then character quests, then clean-up — the platinum pops last, after A Long Road (100% completion). About 200 hours in all, most of it on the 100% checklist.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is possibly missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free roam — there is no mission replay, but no trophy depends on how you play a mission, and cheats disable trophies, so keep them off your trophy run.

    Safe after the story

    All 54 other trophies stay available once the credits roll: every character quest, stranger strand, activity, robbery, collectible and challenge can be finished in free roam.

    Next up

    What to chase next, from your own progress: unfinished story first, then the trophies closest to done, then the next roadmap stage.

    About & sources

    Leonida Platinum is a free, open-source, offline guide. It runs entirely from its own files: no network requests, no account, no ads. Your checklist, counters and notes live only in this browser (localStorage) until you export them.

    Where each fact comes from

    • Exophase — every trophy's official name, description and grade, as PlayStation publishes them. exophase.com — GTA VI trophies
    • PSNProfiles — the progress counts PlayStation tracks for trophies that have one (such as 42 Shooting Range challenges). psnprofiles.com — GTA VI trophies
    • GTABase — the how-to facts behind the guides: where things are, what unlocks them, what is still unconfirmed. gtabase.com — GTA 6 trophy guide

    Every guide on this site is written in our own words for this guide. We cite our sources beside each trophy and claim nothing we have not checked: where a step is not yet confirmed in the game, the guide says so plainly. The official trophy names and descriptions are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used anywhere.

    The official list, at a glance

    55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. 28 are hidden on PlayStation until earned. All single-player; no online trophies.
